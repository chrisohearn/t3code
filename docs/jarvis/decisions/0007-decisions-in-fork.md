# Keep decisions with the fork

Accepted 2026-11-12.

## Decision

Vocabulary and durable decisions for the Jarvis pipeline live under docs/jarvis in Chris's T3 fork.

## Alternatives

Keep them only in ChatGPT instructions, scratch notes, or a separate registry.

## Why

The fork gives the decisions a shared, reviewable home alongside the system they constrain. Personal conversation history is insufficient as the only durable reference.
