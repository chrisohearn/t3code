# Jarvis is the sole chief of staff

Accepted 2026-11-11.

## Decision

Jarvis coordinates shared work. Vision is a worker under Jarvis and receives work by Slack message. Jarvis chooses the model and effort for each T3 task, and decisions needing Chris go to Jarvis in ChatGPT.

## Alternatives

A separate T3 coordinator or two independent chiefs of staff.

## Why

One coordinator keeps ownership and the people-side follow-up in one place instead of splitting them across assistants.
